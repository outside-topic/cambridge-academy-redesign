Certified Professional

A rigorous, internationally recognized credential that validates comprehensive professional competence across knowledge, skills, and ethical standards.

Competency Framework

Body of Professional Competency [CAP - BPC]

The body of professional competency defines the comprehensive knowledge, skills, and attributes that a professional must demonstrate to earn and maintain the CAP Professional Credential. It serves as the foundation for the assessment and certification process.

01

Ethics and Professional Standards

Demonstrating integrity, accountability, and adherence to established codes of conduct and professional ethics in all professional activities.

02

Core Functional Knowledge

Possessing in-depth understanding of fundamental principles, theories, and practices within the chosen professional discipline.

03

Analytical and Problem-Solving Skills

Applying critical thinking, data analysis, and systematic approaches to identify, evaluate, and resolve complex professional challenges.

04

Communication and Interpersonal Skills

Effectively conveying ideas, information, and insights through written, verbal, and visual means while building productive professional relationships.

05

Leadership and Management Competencies

Demonstrating the ability to lead teams, manage resources, drive organizational goals, and inspire professional excellence in others.

Structure

Framework for CAP-Certified Professional Credentials

The framework for CAP-Certified Professional Credentials contains 4 aspects: professional credential level, professional competency level, eligibility for each level, and assessment criteria for each level. This Framework will help with the understanding of the levels of credentials, the eligibility and assessment criteria regarding the nomination, and the preparation for how to achieve the Certified Professional Credential.

Evaluation

Assessment Criteria

The assessment process for CAP Professional Credentials is designed to comprehensively evaluate a candidate's professional competence across all competency areas defined in the Body of Professional Competency. The assessment methodology combines multiple evaluation approaches to ensure validity, reliability, and fairness.

Written Examination Comprehensive assessment of theoretical knowledge and analytical capabilities
Case Study Analysis Practical application of knowledge to real-world professional scenarios
Professional Portfolio Review Evaluation of documented professional achievements and contributions
Professional Reference Verification Validation of professional conduct and competence by qualified referees

(ACP)

Associate Certified Professional

Associate Certified Professional is the level-1 Credential. It is expected that the incumbent applying for this credential should be a Learner & Practitioner [LP] for each of the five criteria.
